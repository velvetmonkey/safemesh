# Why SafeMesh is BSL 1.1

SafeMesh is licensed under the Business Source License 1.1 (BUSL-1.1), converting
to the Apache License 2.0 on the Change Date (2030-07-08).

## Why not permissive (MIT / Apache)?
SafeMesh is verified coordination infrastructure. Fully permissive licensing would
let it be enclosed in commercial products with no path to sustain the verification
work. We want the verified core to stay open and to fund its own hardening.

## Why not AGPL?
AGPL's network copyleft is powerful, but it triggers blanket procurement bans at
many companies -- exactly the mesh / IoT / edge / embedded adopters who benefit
most from verified coordination primitives. It limits adoption without cleanly
capturing commercial value.

## Why BSL 1.1?
BSL puts the friction where it belongs -- on commercial production use -- without
viral copyleft:
- Free for non-commercial use (personal, academic, research, evaluation, development).
- Commercial production use requires a commercial license.
- Every version automatically converts to Apache 2.0 four years after release, so
  the commons is guaranteed, just delayed.

This matches the goal: broad low-friction use, a clear commercial path, and an
eventual open-source guarantee.
