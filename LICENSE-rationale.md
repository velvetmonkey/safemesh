# SafeMesh licensing history

SafeMesh was licensed under BUSL-1.1 before this change. Ben Cassie relicensed
it to Apache-2.0 on 2026-09-05.

A small primitive library that people are meant to embed should not carry a
commercial-use gate. SafeMesh is now open source under Apache-2.0. See LICENSE
for the terms and NOTICE for attribution.
